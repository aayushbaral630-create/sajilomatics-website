Privacy & Cookies

THE SHORT VERSION

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Our analytics are cookie-free. The only cookies on this site come from the optional Calendly booking calendar, and it only loads if you allow it.

What we collect

Anonymous visit statistics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages are useful. It sets no cookies, doesn't store your IP address, and can't identify you across sites.

What you send us. If you email us, message us on WhatsApp, or book a call, we receive the details you choose to share and use them only to reply to you and do the work you ask for.

Cookies

The booking calendar on our Contact page is provided by Calendly, which sets its own cookies to run the scheduler. It loads only after you choose Accept all or click Load calendar. If you choose Essential only, no cookies are set and you can reach us by email or WhatsApp instead.

Your choice is saved in your browser so we don't ask again. You can change it any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

Third-party services

To load quickly, the site fetches fonts from Fontshare and Google Fonts and animation code from Cloudflare and jsDelivr. These requests share your IP address with those providers, as any web request does, but set no cookies. The site is hosted on Vercel.

Your rights and contact

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Write to info@sajilomatics.com and we'll reply within a few working days.
